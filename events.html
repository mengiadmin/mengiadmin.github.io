THU 12.11 Steinalda

Tickets

Thursday, November 12th
STEINALDA
doors 19:30 / show 20:00
2500kr / 2000kr students / pay what u can

Steinalda er hljómsveit sem leikur tónlist eftir Guðmund Stein Gunnarsson og gerði meðal annars garðinn frægann með lögunum um Landvættina fjóra árið 2021. Mengi hefur löngum verið heimavöllur Steinöldu og hyggst hún nú flytja þar verk sem hafa einungis verið flutt lengst upp í Árbæ en verða nú loksins frumflutt í hundrað og einum. Þau heita 'Hugleiðingar um sameiginlegann grundvöll' og 'Hugleiðingar um mikilvægi áningarstaða'. Þá er aldrei að vita nema við þetta bætist nýjir eða gamlir slagarar eða að tekið verði við óskalögum úr verkasafni sveitarinnar. Sveitin ætlar að fylgja Landvættunum fjórum eftir á næstunni með holskeflu af nýjum hljómplötum. Sveitina skipa ásamt Guðmundi Steini, Andrés Þór Þorvarðarson, Ásthildur Ákadóttir, Óskar Magnússon, Páll Ivan frá Eiðum, Steinunn Vala Pálsdóttir og Þórunn Björnsdóttir. Þau spila öll á alls konar.

-----

Steinalda is an ensemble that focuses on the work of composer Guðmundur Steinn Gunnarsson. It is highly experimental yet leaving you with microtonal melodies you'll be humming all day long. The ensemble (or band) did a record called 'Landvættirnar fjórar' in 2021 and is soon going to follow it up with a string of new releases. Some of the works have been dedicated to old gas stations or video rentals or other Reykjavík landmarks that do not exist anymore. They will play two such pieces along with newer or older material and possibly take requests from their repertoire. The group consists of: Andrés Þór Þorvarðarson, Ásthildur Ákadóttir, Guðmundur Steinn Gunnarsson, Óskar Magnússon, Páll Ivan frá Eiðum, Steinunn Vala Pálsdóttir and Þórunn Björnsdóttir. They all play all kinds of things.

THU 19.11 Andervel

ANDERVEL

2500kr / 2000kr students / pay what u can

Earnest, intimate, heartfelt, delicate: Andervel is the music project of indie-folk artist José Luis Anderson. Born in Mexico, based in Iceland, Anderson creates a unique brand of folk music, seen through the lens of multicultural identity. Andervel draws from a broad lineage of lyric-driven songwriting rooted in the folk tradition, creating intimate, expressive works whose emotional directness continues to resonate in live performances. Defined by a timeless voice and carefully crafted lyrics moving seamlessly between Spanish, Icelandic, and English.

“A breathtaking folk composition that drifts in like a warm summer breeze and settles somewhere deep in the chest.” Atwood

On Ironclad and Palm Trees, Andervel’s debut album, the musician unveils his most fully realised and culturally interwoven work to date. A folk-leaning, multilingual record shaped by migration, memory and the meeting of two homelands. Where the warmth of Mexico meets the windswept quiet of Iceland

THU 26.11 HIST OG

Tickets

Thursday, November 26th
HIST OG
doors 19:30 / show 20:00
2500kr / 2000kr students / pay what u can

Eiríkur Orri Ólafsson: trumpet, electronics
Róberta Andersen: guitar, bass, synth
Magnús Trygvason Elíassen: drums, percussion

Founded in 2017 to distil a murky blend of jazz and electronic music, hist og is a trio featuring Eiríkur Orri Ólafsson on trumpet and electronics, Róberta Andersen on guitar, bass and synth, and Magnús Trygvason Eliassen on drums and percussion. Through the lens of their shared musical language, they explore a Day-Glo lit ruminative world of improvisation, jazz and electronic music.

Hist og has released four albums, days of tundra (2019), hits of (2020), holy ghost of (2023), and Hist Highness (2026). the first three records have received wide praise, harvesting six nominations to the Icelandic Music Awards, a seat on the venerated Kraumur list in 2019 and 2020. Days of Tundra was named "improv of the year" by Morgunblaðið in 2019. Music critic Arnar Eggert Thoroddsen wrote "smashing stuff … every song is rigidly stamped by the seal of this super-trio." Kevin Whitlock of Jazzwise wrote this about their 2024 Reykjavík Jazz Festival appearance:

"they are at once thunderous, producing huge walls of sound, and spacey; while also laying down serious grooves. […] They'd go down a storm at a venue like London's Café OTO – let's hope they visit the UK soon."

The members of hist og have worked with countless outfits over the years, such as múm, Sin Fang, Amiina, Sigur Rós, Kira Kira, Seabear and others. they have also collaborated with supreme jazz entities such as ADHD, Hilmar Jensson, Skúli Sverrisson and Mógil.

------

Tríóið hist og var stofnað í lok árs 2017 til þess að rugla saman reitum raf-og djasstónlistar. Tríóið skipa Eiríkur Orri Ólafsson, sem leikur á trompet, trommuheila og hljómboð, Róberta Andersen á gítar, bassa og hljómborð og Magnús Trygvason Eliassen á trommur og slagverk. Meðlimir tríósins beina stækkunarglerinu að sameiginlegum snertifleti sínum, með innhverfri, úthverfri og slagþungri blöndu af djass, raf-, og spunatónlist sem kemur víða við.

hist og á að baki fjórar plötur, Days of Tundra (2019), hits of (2020), holy ghost of (2023), og Hist Highness (2026). Fyrri þrjár plöturnar hlutu mikið lof og hefur hljómsveitin landað þremur tilnefningum til Íslensku Tónlistarverðlaunanna í djassflokki bæði árin, sem og sæti á Kraumslistanum 2019 og "Spuni Ársins" sama ár hjá Morgunblaðinu. Um hits of skrifaði Arnar Eggert Thoroddsen hjá Morgunblaðinu ma. "Hörkustöff … stimpill þessa ofurtríós kirfilega á öllum smíðum." Allar plöturnar hafa hlotið sæti á Kraumslistanum. Breska tímaritið Jazzwise hafði þetta að segja um tónleika þeirra á Jazzhátíð í ágúst síðastliðnum:

"they are at once thunderous, producing huge walls of sound, and spacey; while also laying down serious grooves. […] They'd go down a storm at a venue like London's Café OTO – let's hope they visit the UK soon."

Meðlimir hist og hafa starfað um árabil með hinum ýmsustu hljómsveitum, svo sem múm, amiinu og Sin Fang, Sigur Rós, Kiru Kiru, Benna Hemm Hemm og Seabear, ásamt ótal samstarfsverkefna á sviði djasstónlistar með ADHD, Hilmari Jenssyni, Skúla Sverrissyni og Mógil.

Sláturtíð 2026
27.–29. nóvember 2026 · Mengi, Óðinsgötu 2, og Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík

Sláturtíð snýr aftur með þriggja daga dagskrá af framsækinni nýrri tónlist: hreyfinótur, frjáls spuni og nýtt tónverk að tilefni aldarafmælis orgels Fríkirkjunnar í Reykjavík.
Sláturtíð er uppskeruhátíð S.L.Á.T.U.R. (Samtaka listrænt ágengra tónsmiða umhverfis Reykjavík). Hátíðin var fyrst haldin árið 2009 og hefur verið haldin margoft síðan.
Sláturtíð 2026 er haldin í samstarfi við Skólahljómsveit Vesturbæjar og Miðbæjar, SVoM, Fríkirkjuna í Reykjavík og Mengi, Óðinsgötu.

Sunnudagur 29. nóvember · Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík Áki Ásgeirsson: 105° (frumflutningur)

Árið 1926 var Sauer-orgel sett upp í Fríkirkjunni, smíðað af orgelsmiðju Wilhelms Sauer undir stjórn Pauls Walcker. Tónleikarnir fagna aldarafmæli orgelsins með frumflutningi á 105°, nýju verki fyrir tölvustýrt kirkjuorgel, leikið með MIDI.
105° tilheyrir langri tónverkaröð Áka þar sem talið er niður um eina gráðu í hverjum mánuði. Röðin hófst með 360° í ágúst 2005 og lýkur með 0° í ágúst 2035.

Sláturtíð 2026

27–29 November 2026 · Mengi, Óðinsgata 2, and Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík

Sláturtíð returns with a three-day programme of adventurous new music: animated scores, free improvisation and a new work marking the centenary of the organ of Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík.
Sláturtíð is the festival of S.L.Á.T.U.R. (Samtök listrænt ágengra tónsmiða umhverfis Reykjavík – the Association of Artistically Obtrusive Composers around Reykjavík). The festival was first held in 2009 and has returned many times since.
Sláturtíð 2026 is held in collaboration with Skólahljómsveit Vesturbæjar og Miðbæjar (SVoM), Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík and Mengi.

Young musicians in SVoM perform older and newer works by members of S.L.Á.T.U.R., written as animated scores using computer graphics. S.L.Á.T.U.R. members have been known for such works ever since the collective was founded in 2005. Now 10th-grade students in SVoM get the chance to discover this music and bring graphic notation to life.

Áki and Ingi take the stage with a variety of instruments, both acoustic and electronic. Together they create music on the spot in free improvisation. Both have designed their own instruments, and for many years have played a range of wind instruments and composed experimental music.

Sunday 29 November · Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík
Áki Ásgeirsson: 105° (premiere)

In 1926, a Sauer organ was installed in Fríkirkjan, built by the Wilhelm Sauer organ workshop under Paul Walcker. The concert celebrates the organ’s centenary with the premiere of 105°, a new work for computer-controlled church organ, played via MIDI.
105° belongs to Áki’s long series of works counting down one degree each month. The series began with 360° in August 2005 and ends with 0° in August 2035.

About the partners:

Sláturtíð 2026 is a collaboration between S.L.Á.T.U.R., SVoM, Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík and Mengi. Áki and Ingi Garðar are this year’s festival directors.

S.L.Á.T.U.R. is an experimental composers’ collective founded in Reykjavík in 2005, known for animated notation, interactivity and experiments with sound.

SVoM is the school band of the Vesturbær and Miðbær districts of Reykjavík.
Fríkirkjan í Reykjavík stands by Tjörnin in central Reykjavík and is home to the Sauer organ from 1926.

Mengi is an arts venue at Óðinsgata 2 presenting experimental music, performance and visual art.
