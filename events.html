THU 22.10 Strandir (IS/DE)

Tickets

Thursday, October 22nd
STRANDIR (IS/DE)
doors 19:30 / show 20:00
2500kr / 2000kr students / pay what u can

Tumi Torfason : trumpet
Tobias Link : trombone
Daníel Helgason : guitar & electronics
Stefan Vidal-Schneider : drums & electronics
Strandir is a German/Icelandic quartet that has been working intermittently for just over a year. A series of coincidences unexpectedly brought the group together to discover a new sonic landscape to explore. A vast space spanning everything from the sparse to the dense, from the acoustic to the electronic and from the improvised to the written. Since their debut as a trio in Mengi last year they have since added a fourth member, performed in Denmark and Germany, recorded material that will soon be released and found their collective voice through free improvisation sparked by simple themes. The group returns to Mengi for a new rendition of their sonic image.

SAT 24.10 Jesper Pedersen: 50 Years On Planet Earth

Tickets

Saturday, October 24th
JESPER PEDERSEN: 50 YEARS ON PLANET EARTH
doors 19:30 / show 20:00
2500kr / 2000kr students / pay what u can

Jesper Pedersen is celebrating 50 years on Planet Earth with a concert at Mengi.

For this half-century celebration, Jesper will be joined by Ása Önnu Ólafsdóttir (Asalaus), Berglind María Tómasdóttir, Helga Björg Jóhönnu Árnardóttir and Katie Buckley for an evening of music, sound, improvisation and collaboration.

Expect electronics, acoustic instruments, strange sounds, unexpected combinations and probably a few surprises.

Jesper is a Reykjavík-based composer, sound artist, performer and educator. Working with electronic sound since the 1990s, his practice spans electroacoustic music, experimental composition, sound art, installation and improvisation. He performs on modular synthesizers, electronics, theremin and unconventional instruments, exploring texture, noise, drone and spatial sound, both solo and in collaboration with others.
